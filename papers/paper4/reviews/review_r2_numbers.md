# Paper 4 v2 — round-2 numbers and claims audit

Audited file: `Revision_v2/Paper4/Manuscript_filled.md` (with the table captions from `build.py` TABLE_CAPTIONS and the figure captions/scripts from `build.py` / `make_figures.py`).
Ground truth: `Papers pipeline/experiments/paper4_federated/results_v2/<ds>/<exp>/seed<r>.json` and `<ds>/tuned.json`. `_bn_superseded` was ignored.
Method: I recomputed everything with my own scripts, not with `compute_values.py`. I re-derived each validation-selected test F1 from `history` (earliest argmax of `val_f1`) and each local-only score from `per_client[*].history`. I used t-based 95% CIs and paired t-tests over shared seeds, and added Holm corrections and exact Wilcoxon tests as extra checks. ε was recomputed with an independent RDP implementation for the Poisson-subsampled Gaussian (integer orders 2–256, Balle et al. conversion). Test and validation set sizes were checked by integrality of `test_acc × N` and `val_acc × N`. No file was modified, and no model was loaded or trained.
